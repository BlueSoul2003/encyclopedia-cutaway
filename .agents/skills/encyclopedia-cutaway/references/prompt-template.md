# Prompt template

Replace fields and remove irrelevant lines. This is a scaffold, not a fixed composition or palette.

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: [aspect, intended viewing size], original illustrated encyclopedia cutaway plate.
Input images: [each role: style reference / factual evidence / edit target; omit if absent].
Primary request: Explain [subject] for [audience], emphasizing [one mechanism or relationship].

Art direction: Classic printed science encyclopedia, meticulous hand-painted gouache/watercolor, fine ink edges with slight natural variation, matte modeled materials, delicate brushwork and pigment pooling. [Material palette]. No glossy CGI or flat icon-card infographic.
Background: [white / full-page colored paper / restrained environmental wash], chosen for [subject-specific reason]. Main and supporting tones [colors]. Carry the ground to all page edges and caption margins. [Dark / pale] text and fine leaders with strong contrast; quiet caption areas, no white cards.

Geometry: [recognizable outside form, selected cut planes, part positions]. Preserve spatial relationships, remove only necessary exterior.
Mechanism: [source → stages → endpoint, or relevant biological/geological relationships].
Branches: [purpose, direction, endpoint, or omit].
Critical constraints: [physical facts that generation must preserve]. Every pipe or shaft has a meaningful endpoint; arrows follow the actual connection.

Layout: One dominant coherent cutaway, [caption count] readable compact captions around it with leaders to real parts. [Classic: modest title, narrow arrows / teaching: fewer captions and larger type]. Keep essential extremities inside the frame.
Living accents: [none / a few quiet figures or creatures and their activity]. Place [shared scene at plausible scale / separate margin vignette]. Same hand-painted medium, outside machinery, no new labels, no overlap with technical content.

Text, verbatim, in [language and script]:
Title: "[title]"
Subtitle: "[optional]"
01: "[heading]" / "[one concise function]"
[Meaningful callouts only.]
Legend: "[if needed]"
Scope footer: "[if needed]"
Preserve accents, script, punctuation and natural wording. No tiny invented filler or unsupported numeric claims.

Avoid: [topic-specific errors], decorative caption cards, meaningless gears, futuristic glow, logos, watermark.
```

For edits, name the concrete location and replacement, then preserve validated text, layout, connections, and texture. Example: extend only the tank suction to below the waterline near the floor; leave the collection inlet and all captions untouched. Inspect again: adding a correct new pipe may not remove an incorrect old one.
